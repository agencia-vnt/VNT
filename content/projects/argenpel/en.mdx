## The context

ARGEN PEL rewinds and converts tissue paper for industrial and household use.
Its range includes products that look similar at first glance but differ in
quality, packaging, weight and other specifications that matter when making
an enquiry.

The project brought that information into a website that also introduced
the company. Company copy, catalogue sheets and photography needed to form a
clear path: get to know the supplier, find a product family and gather the
details needed to start a commercial conversation.

## The scope

We developed the home page, company section, catalogue pages and contact
page. Navigation, images and product cards adapt to desktop and mobile.
Products are presented for enquiries; purchases and commercial terms are
discussed directly with the company.

### Organising around what visitors need

The catalogue has four categories: toilet paper, interfolded towels, roll
towels and industrial rolls. Nine product records bring together their
specifications and photographs.

We kept specific fields for the characteristics available in the source
material: quality, units per package, weights, packaging and options such as
perforation or dimensions. Each card displays the information available for
that product, without filling unknown fields with generic descriptions.

### Preserving the catalogue's distinctions

A list of weights and a list of package sizes do not establish that every
combination is available. We therefore kept those options separate instead
of turning them into purchase variants that the catalogue does not confirm.
Photographs are not assigned to a quality solely by the colour of their
packaging, either.

Information is maintained in structured files, and product cards are
generated from that same data. Specifications can be reviewed without
maintaining another copy inside every page.

### Showing the company alongside its products

Photography of the plant, stock and production accompanies the company
presentation. The company section includes a video of the industrial
process, with a still cover and interaction to pause playback. When a
visitor prefers reduced motion, the video waits for an action before
starting.

The contact page brings together the location and links to WhatsApp, email
and Instagram, allowing visitors to continue their enquiries through the
company's existing channels.

## The result

The completed website brings the company presentation and informational
catalogue into one structure. Product data is kept separate from its
presentation to make later reviews of specifications and photography
easier.

Commercial enquiries go through the company's direct channels. We do not
yet present measured commercial results: tracking needs to distinguish
catalogue visits, clicks to contact channels and enquiries actually received.
