## Showing how Luquisound works

Luquisound is a vehicle audio, soundproofing, and customization workshop.
Photographs and videos explain something a service list alone cannot show: how
equipment is integrated, the finish of an installation, and the work behind it.

VNT carried out the complete web design and development, using brand assets
supplied by Luquisound. The project focused on organizing the services, showing
the workshop's work, and making it easier to start an inquiry.

## A route from services to completed work

The website is organized into home, services, work, and contact. Services have
three entry points —audio, soundproofing, and customization— and connect to
related work. This lets a specific installation explain a broader category
before someone asks about their own vehicle.

The gallery groups photographs and videos by job, with filters and a detail
view. Each collection lets visitors explore an installation, its process, and
its finishes in the same context. Clips play manually, so people can choose
what to watch as they explore the work.

## Imagery that adapts to the device

The opening section uses different framing on desktop and phones. A horizontal
version shows the workshop on a wide screen; vertical footage supports browsing
on a phone. We included fallback images and an alternative for people who
prefer reduced motion.

The same attention to format carries through navigation and work details:
visitors should be able to explore the content regardless of screen size.

## From browsing to an inquiry

The website brings together the workshop's contact details and access to
WhatsApp to continue the conversation about a specific job. Services explain
the possibilities, while the gallery provides examples before that contact.

The delivery comprises the complete website and its adaptation to desktop and
phones. The internal quotation tool we developed for the same workshop has its
[own case study](/en/proyectos/luquisound-cotizador), with a different objective
and usage flow.
