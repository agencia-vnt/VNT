## The problem

AKDemia is Facundo Massó's driving school in Villa Urquiza, Buenos Aires.
Before this project, it had no website: just an Instagram account, with content
published without a defined system or direction.

The deeper problem was its positioning. Driving schools tend to use the same
words: easy, fast, in record time, guaranteed pass. None addresses the real
reason someone might put off learning to drive for years: fear. That left room
for a different approach.

## What we did

### The brand

We built a complete system around the logo: a palette with tonal scales and
cool neutrals, typography, graphic elements, tone of voice and usage rules.
The palette defines each colour's purpose and how to combine it across the
brand's materials.

The decision that guided everything else was about language. The positioning
became **overcoming the fear of driving**, rather than getting a licence. The
brand manual explicitly lists the words the school does not use: *easy*,
*fast*, *in record time*, *cheap*, *guaranteed pass*. A list of words to avoid
is easier to apply than three paragraphs about brand personality. The slogan
became **"Paso a paso" — "Step by step"**.

### The landing page

A static website, without a CMS or backend, provides what someone needs before
getting in touch: the available lessons, where they take place, who teaches
them, the steps involved and what happens in the first lesson. Contact happens
through WhatsApp, where the school handles those conversations.

We included `LocalBusiness` and `FAQPage` structured data to describe the
business and its frequently asked questions to search engines.

### The map

The first question for anyone looking for a driving school is whether lessons
are available in their neighbourhood.

All ten neighbourhoods appear as text as well as on the map. The list lets
visitors check the area without relying on the map, while the WhatsApp button
offers a direct way to confirm an address with the school.

The map displays neighbourhood boundaries from Buenos Aires Data and keeps
the source attribution visible.

### The content

Instagram remains the school's main channel, so we created a system rather
than a handful of posts. It has four content pillars: information, education,
community and humour. Each has a purpose, a weekly schedule, call-to-action
rules and a bank of ideas to draw from without starting from scratch every
week.

<Figure

The two trust pillars, information and education, explain why people choose
the school. The two relationship pillars, community and humour, give them a
reason to recommend it. The balance between them guides the content.

## The result

The site is live, and the brand is documented in a manual the school can use
without depending on us.

We do not yet have measured results: the website is recent and there was no
previous site to compare it with. The next step is to measure WhatsApp
enquiries from the website, which neighbourhoods appear in searches, and
whether community and humour posts sustain the reach currently achieved by
informational content.
