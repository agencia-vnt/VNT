## A foundation for product discovery

Aleatur brings together fragrances and personal care products. The project
organises that range into a website where visitors can browse, identify
products of interest and prepare an enquiry. The commercial conversation
continues on WhatsApp, where prices and availability are confirmed.

VNT designed and developed the web experience: an introductory landing
page, a separate catalog, shareable product pages and a personal selection.
Visitors can explore the overall range or go straight to a specific product.

## From catalog to product page

The home page presents categories and selected highlights. The catalog
provides the discovery tools: search by product, brand or code, category
and brand filters, sorting options and page navigation.

Filters are reflected in the page address. Opening a product page and
returning preserves the browsing context, so comparing alternatives does
not require starting over.

Each product has its own address, an expandable image and actions to
share, save or enquire. Descriptive information appears when it is
available in the catalog source; the structure allows those details to
be added progressively.

## Choosing before getting in touch

Visitors can save several products and collect them in “Mi selección”
(My selection). The list stays in the browser and lets them remove items
or return to browsing before making an enquiry. The WhatsApp action
prepares a message with the selected products, which the visitor reviews
and sends through that channel.

## A website in use and still evolving

The interface uses dark surfaces, sans-serif typography and consistently
presented product photographs. Images have variants for different screen
sizes, while product pages let visitors enlarge the detail available in
each source photograph.

The catalog starts from a master spreadsheet and is incorporated through
a reviewed update. That separation allows work on data and photography
without every edit immediately changing the website.

The website is already in use and continues to evolve. Its structure supports
new products and revised product pages as the catalog data and photographs
are updated.
