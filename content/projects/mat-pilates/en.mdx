## The context

MAT Pilates is a studio in Canning offering mat-based classes and movement
in a warm environment. For someone arriving from Instagram, the website
needed to answer the questions that come before a first visit: what the
studio offers, which classes are available, when they take place and how
to get in touch.

The landing page has supported the business since its opening. We organised
the public presentation around the studio's confirmed information and a path
from initial interest to direct contact.

## The scope

We developed a landing page introducing the Hot Mat method, a class
catalogue, weekly schedules, a studio gallery, location details and WhatsApp
links. VNT designed and developed the complete website. The studio supplied
its brandbook, developed by **CONTRAST3**, as the identity reference.

### Turning the identity into an interface

The palette, logos and Neue Montreal typography form the visual system's
foundation. We defined their use across headings, body text, buttons and
backgrounds, with adjustments for desktop and mobile.

Photography shows the studio and its equipment, while expandable sections
let visitors explore information without displaying every detail at once.
Motion supports those interactions and respects the device's reduced-motion
preference.

### Connecting classes and schedules

The catalogue brings together four classes, each with a description,
intensity and environment. Each class includes its days and times. Visitors
can move from a class to the weekly schedule and from the schedule back to
the class details.

The same schedule data supplies both views. Desktop uses a weekly table;
mobile uses expandable days. Changing a time does not require updating
multiple independent copies of the information.

### Keeping classes and schedules connected

Classes and schedules have a structured representation on the website.
Descriptions and links from the timetable use consistent class names so
visitors can connect each class with its times and review its details.
That shared structure also supports updates when the schedule changes.

### Ending the journey with a conversation

Each class's call to action opens WhatsApp with the selected class name.
The gallery, address and directions link complete the journey before
visitors make an enquiry.

Arranging the first class continues directly with the studio through that
channel. The landing page brings the public information together; bookings
and payments are coordinated through the business's own channels.

## The result

The first website stage is complete and has been in use since the studio
opened. It brings together the public information and lets visitors explore
the offer across screen sizes. The management platform considered during
discovery was not developed.

The next follow-up will look at enquiries from the landing page and which
classes visitors ask about. There are no measured results yet that would
allow us to attribute bookings or studio growth to the website.
