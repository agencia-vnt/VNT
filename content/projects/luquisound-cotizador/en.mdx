## A tool for preparing each proposal

Luquisound combines equipment, installation, and accessories according to the
work each vehicle needs. We developed an internal quotation tool designed for
phone use to prepare these custom proposals.

VNT carried out the complete web design and development of the tool, using
brand assets supplied by Luquisound. The goal was to connect line-item editing,
calculations, and the image shared with the customer within a single flow.

## The quotation as the interface

The main screen shows the proposal. Tapping the title or a line item opens the
editor, while the preview keeps the graphic composition of the final file.
This relationship lets the workshop review content in context and keeps
equipment, installation, and totals together.

Audio categories help organize the items, and custom rows can also be added.
Equipment is separated from installation and accessories. Each line has its own
quantity, price, and currency, with descriptions that can be edited for the
specific job.

## Calculations that preserve each amount's meaning

The tool supports pesos and dollars within the same proposal. It adds amounts
in their own currency and calculates both totals using the displayed
exchange-rate reference. A pending price, an explicit zero, and an item
included at no charge keep their different meanings throughout editing and
image preparation.

The current quotation is recovered in the same browser. This continuity
supports editing without turning the tool into a quotation archive: its
purpose is to prepare the current proposal. Luquisound's fixed templates remain
a separate resource.

## An image ready to share

The final step generates a PNG containing the proposal, its items, and its
totals. Editing controls and hints stay out of the file. The image can be
downloaded or shared through the options supported by the device.
