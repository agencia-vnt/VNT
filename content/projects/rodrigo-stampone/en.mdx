## The context

Rodrigo Stampone's activity brings together events, tickets, table enquiries
and contact links. Each date has its own information: artist, venue, promoter
and ticket destination. The website needed to gather these destinations in
a page that could be shared and kept up to date.

The work covered the design and development of the landing page, together
with an administration dashboard for managing its content. The available
visual material for Rodrigo and 4SIDE formed the basis of the presentation.

## The schedule comes first

Upcoming events occupy the first section of the page. Each card shows the
date, artist, venue and promoter. The Tickets link opens the corresponding
ticket website; when a location has been added, the venue name opens it in
Maps.

Table enquiries have a separate link that opens WhatsApp with a message
identifying the event. Visitors can choose to buy a ticket or start an
enquiry with the relevant date already included in the conversation.

## A presentation adapted to phones

The interface combines a dark background, the 4SIDE sign and a hierarchy
that separates dates, names and actions. The sign includes a lighting
animation, with a still alternative for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

The schedule is followed by Rodrigo's profile, portrait and permanent
links. The layout adjusts to a phone screen while keeping each event's
actions within its card.

## Managing content from a dashboard

The Sanity dashboard supports creating and editing events, choosing venues
and promoters, featuring dates and controlling visibility. It also brings
together profile editing, permanent links and a special link that can remain
available independently of the schedule.

The administration interface distinguishes drafts from published content.
Events expire automatically after their date, with an option to set a
different expiry. Upcoming and past events are separated in the dashboard
to make reviewing content easier.

## The result

The landing page is complete, delivered and in use. It brings the schedule
and contact channels together at a dedicated address, with content
administration to support changes in programming.

Future tracking can measure ticket link visits and enquiries started from
each date; we are
not yet presenting conversion figures or sales attributed to the website.
